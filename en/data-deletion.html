Data deletion

How to have the data that SocialHQ has stored deleted. SocialHQ is an internal tool that SEDOVI uses to publish to its own accounts.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Ask for deletion

Choose one of these two ways:

  1. Email developer@sedovi.nl with the subject "Data deletion". Say which platform and which account it is about.
  2. Or remove SocialHQ's access in the platform's settings. Look for "Apps and websites" or "Connected apps" and remove SocialHQ.

What we do next

Within 30 days we delete the stored access tokens and all data of that account in SocialHQ: account id, handle, post links and numbers. If you emailed us, we confirm when it is done.

Posts that are already on a platform are removed on that platform itself.

Website data

Want a message from the contact form deleted? Email developer@sedovi.nl as well. The privacy policy has more details.
