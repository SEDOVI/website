Web app · 13 languages · servers in Germany

Soevra

Measures how sovereign a cloud service is under the European Commission's Cloud Sovereignty Framework. A provider answers 48 questions and gets a score from 0 to 100 and a SEAL level from 0 to 4.

  • Sign-in with an emailed code, no password
  • Own servers in Germany, with an encrypted backup every night
  • Reporting form and handling under the Digital Services Act

// process

How a project runs.

  1. Intro call

    A free 30-minute call. You explain what you want to build and who it is for.

  2. Proposal

    Within a week you get a fixed price, a schedule and a list of what is and isn't included.

  3. Build

    You try in-between versions on your phone or in your browser, and your feedback goes into the next round.

  4. Launch

    SEDOVI puts your product in the stores or online and stays available for maintenance.

How is this faster and cheaper than an agency?

SEDOVI builds with AI tools for code, tests and reviews. Routine work goes faster, so you pay for fewer hours. Every change goes through automated tests and a review before it goes live.

Who do I work with?

The developer who builds your product. No account manager or project manager in between.

Who owns the code?

You do, once the last invoice is paid. You get the source code and the accounts for the stores, hosting and payments.

Where is the data stored?

In the EU where possible. Soevra, for example, runs on servers in Germany. The proposal says where your data will live.

I already have an app or website. Can SEDOVI take it over?

Yes. You first get a short review of the code: what works, what needs fixing first and what that costs.

What do you want to build?

Book a free 30-minute video call on Google Meet. You pick a time that suits you in the calendar.
