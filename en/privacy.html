Privacy policy

This policy covers the website sedovi.nl and SocialHQ, an internal tool of SEDOVI.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Who we are

SEDOVI Development B.V. in Spijkenisse, the Netherlands (KvK 42084959) is responsible for the data on this page. Send privacy questions to developer@sedovi.nl.

The website sedovi.nl

  • Contact form. You enter your name, email address, your company if you want, the type of project and your message. The form sends this as an email to SEDOVI, through our hosting provider Strato. We only use it to reply to you and keep your message as long as your question needs it.
  • Limiting spam. The form counts how many messages come from the same IP address per hour. For this we keep an unreadable (hashed) version of your IP address, never the address itself.
  • Booking a call. That button opens the Google Calendar booking page. What you enter there goes to SEDOVI and to Google. Google uses it under its own privacy policy.
  • Fonts and hosting. The site runs on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. This means GitHub and Google see your IP address when you open the site.
  • No cookies or analytics. The site sets no cookies and does not track visitors.

SocialHQ

SocialHQ is an internal tool of SEDOVI. SEDOVI uses it to publish its own content, for example about apps such as DutchPrep, to SEDOVI's own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Pinterest. Other people or companies cannot use SocialHQ.

SocialHQ only handles this data:

  • access tokens for SEDOVI's accounts, stored in the macOS Keychain on a SEDOVI computer;
  • account ids and handles of those accounts;
  • ids and links of published posts;
  • public numbers per post: views, likes, comments and shares.

SocialHQ does not collect data about other people and SEDOVI does not sell data. We share nothing with others, except with the platform where a post is published.

How long we keep data

  • Access tokens: until the account is disconnected.
  • Post records and numbers: as long as the account is connected.

The Data deletion page explains how to have everything deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can ask which data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Email developer@sedovi.nl. You get an answer within one month. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
