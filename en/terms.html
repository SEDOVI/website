What SocialHQ is

SocialHQ is an internal tool of SEDOVI. SEDOVI uses it to publish its own content, for example about apps such as DutchPrep, to SEDOVI's own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Pinterest. Other people or companies cannot use SocialHQ.

Who uses it

Only SEDOVI uses SocialHQ, and only for SEDOVI's own accounts. You cannot sign up for it. When publishing, SEDOVI follows the rules of each platform.
